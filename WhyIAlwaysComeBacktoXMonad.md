## 🧠 Why I Always Come Back to XMonad

I’ve tried many window managers.

Some were faster.
Some were prettier.
Some were easier.

But I always come back to **XMonad**.

### Reasons (learned the hard way)

- **It is configuration, not customization**
  - One file
  - One language
  - One mental model

- **If it compiles, it works**
  - No hidden state
  - No GUI toggles silently changing behavior
  - No “why did this break?” moments

- **Layouts are logic, not magic**
  - Windows behave exactly as described
  - No surprises
  - No heuristics guessing what I want

- **It scales with complexity**
  - Simple when simple is enough
  - Powerful when things get serious
  - Does not fight you when your setup grows

- **It respects muscle memory**
  - Keybindings don’t change
  - Behavior doesn’t drift
  - Years of usage still apply

### The real reason

XMonad doesn’t try to be smart.

It expects **you** to be.

And that’s exactly what I want from a window manager.
